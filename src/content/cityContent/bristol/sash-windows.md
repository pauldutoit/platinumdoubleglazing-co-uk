## Frequently asked questions

**Can I fit double-glazed sash windows on a listed or conservation-area property in Bristol?**
Often yes, but clifton and Redland have extensive conservation area coverage, meaning window replacements often need to match the original glazing pattern, so slimline double-glazed units that replicate the original glazing bars are usually the way through rather than a standard modern sash.

**Is it better to repair or replace old sash windows?**
If the timber frame itself is sound, draught-proofing and mechanism repairs are usually cheaper and can be enough. Full sash replacement makes more sense once the frame has rotted or the sash cords have failed repeatedly.

**How long does sash window replacement take in Bristol?**
A single sash window usually takes half a day including making good the surrounding plaster or woodwork, though full-house jobs on larger period properties take longer.
