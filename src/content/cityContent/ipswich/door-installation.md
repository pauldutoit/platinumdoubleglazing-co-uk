**What's the difference between composite and uPVC doors?**
Composite doors have a solid timber-effect core that's more resistant to warping and generally more secure, while uPVC doors cost less but can flex slightly more over time. Most Ipswich homeowners upgrading a front door go composite; uPVC is more common for less visible back or side doors.
