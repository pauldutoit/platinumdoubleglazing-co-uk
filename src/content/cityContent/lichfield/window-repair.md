## Frequently asked questions

**Is it cheaper to repair a window or replace it?**
Repair is almost always cheaper if the frame itself is sound — a failed seal, hinge or handle repair costs a fraction of a full replacement, which is only worth it if the frame is rotten, warped or beyond economical repair.

**Why has my double glazing misted up between the panes?**
That misting means the gas seal inside the sealed unit has failed and let moist air in — the glass itself is fine, but the unit needs replacing to stop the fogging, which a repair visit can usually do without replacing the whole frame.

**Can you repair a window the same day in Lichfield?**
For urgent issues like a window that won't lock or close securely, we prioritise same or next-day callouts; less urgent jobs like a misted unit replacement are usually booked within a few days.
