**Composite or uPVC - which is better for a front door in Durham?**
Composite doors cost more but hold their shape and colour better over time and generally feel more solid. uPVC is the cheaper option and still performs well if budget is the main concern.

**How secure are modern replacement doors?**

**Can bifold doors be fitted to an existing opening in Durham?**
Usually, yes, though older medieval and Georgian properties sometimes need the opening adjusted slightly since original openings weren't built to modern bifold door widths.
