**Do casement windows need planning permission in Aberdeen?**

**What's the difference between uPVC and timber-effect casement frames?**
uPVC is the lower-maintenance, lower-cost option, while timber-effect composite frames cost more but suit period-adjacent streets where appearance matters more.
