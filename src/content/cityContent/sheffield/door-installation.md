## Frequently asked questions

**What's the difference between composite and uPVC doors?**
Composite doors have a solid timber-effect core that's more resistant to warping and generally more secure, while uPVC doors cost less but can flex slightly more over time. Most Sheffield homeowners upgrading a front door go composite; uPVC is more common for less visible back or side doors.

**How secure are modern replacement doors?**
Multi-point locking systems, reinforced frames and toughened glass panels are all standard on the doors we fit, which is a significant step up from older single-lock doors still found on a lot of older housing in Sheffield.

**Can I get a matching front and back door in Sheffield?**
Yes - most ranges let you match colour and hardware finish across front, back and side doors so they look consistent from both inside and outside the property.
