## Frequently asked questions

**Composite or uPVC - which is better for a front door in Brighton?**
Composite doors cost more but hold their shape and colour better over time and generally feel more solid. uPVC is the cheaper option and still performs well if budget is the main concern.

**How secure are modern replacement doors?**
Most new composite and uPVC doors come with multi-point locking as standard, which is a meaningful security upgrade over an older single-point lock, whatever finish you choose.

**How long does a door installation take?**
A single front or back door is typically a half-day job. A full bifold set across a rear extension takes longer, usually a full day, because of the extra frame and track work.
