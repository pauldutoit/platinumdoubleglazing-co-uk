## Frequently asked questions

**Do casement windows need planning permission in Plymouth?**
Most like-for-like casement replacements do not need planning permission. The main exception is where the Barbican and areas around the Hoe have conservation status protecting their historic character — in that case it is worth a quick check with the council before ordering.

**How long does a casement window installation take?**
A typical Plymouth home with six to eight casement windows is usually finished in a single day by a two-person team, though larger properties or ones with tricky access can take longer.

**What's the difference between uPVC and timber-effect casement frames?**
uPVC is the lower-maintenance, lower-cost option, while timber-effect composite frames cost more but suit period-adjacent streets where appearance matters more.
