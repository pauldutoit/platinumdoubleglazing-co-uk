**Does a bay window replacement in Huddersfield need planning permission?**

**Can you replace just the glass in a bay window rather than the whole frame?**
Sometimes, if the frame is in good structural condition — but on older Victorian stone-built bays the frame and glass are often replaced together since they were built and sealed as one unit.
